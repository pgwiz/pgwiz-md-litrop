# ⚡ 24/7 Automation & Presence Heartbeat

Keep your WhatsApp account active with intelligent automated background tasks.

### Features:
* `.alwaysonline`: Keeps your bot in active presence state with a 15-second socket heartbeat.
* `.autostatus`: Automatically views contact status stories with custom ignore list support.
* `.autoread`: Automatically marks messages as read.
* `.autoreact`: Automatically reacts to incoming messages with dynamic emojis.
* `.autotyping`: Simulates realistic typing presence.
