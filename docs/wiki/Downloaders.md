# 📥 Media & Social Scrapers

Download media from social platforms without watermarks at high speed.

### Commands:
* `.play <song title>`: Search and download YouTube audio as MP3.
* `.song <url / query>`: High-bitrate audio fetcher.
* `.video <url / query>`: Download YouTube HD MP4 video.
* `.tiktok <url>`: Download TikTok video without watermark.
* `.fb <url>`: Download Facebook video clips and reels.
* `.ig <url>`: Download Instagram posts, carousels, and reels.
